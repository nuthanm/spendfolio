📊 CALCULATIONS EXPLAINED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Weighted Average Cost (Fair P&L)
├─ Formula: Total Investment / Total Grams
└─ Example:
   Buy 10g @ ₹5000 = ₹50,000
   Buy 15g @ ₹5100 = ₹76,500
   Average = (50,000 + 76,500) / 25 = ₹5,060/gram

Profit/Loss on Sale
├─ Formula: (Sale Grams × Sale Rate) - (Sale Grams × Avg Cost)
└─ Example:
   Sell 5g @ ₹5300
   P&L = (5 × 5300) - (5 × 5060) = +₹1,200 profit ✅

Goal Progress
├─ Formula: (Current Grams / Goal Grams) × 100
└─ Example:
   Current: 25g, Goal: 100g = 25% complete
